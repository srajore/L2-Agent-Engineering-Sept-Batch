2The loop, and the harness wrapped around it

act and verify are the loop — they can only decide "try again." Everything that decides "stop trying, permanently" is the harness, shown here as the dashed boundary and the three exits.

Any one of these three ends the run. None of them can override another — that split between "the loop's verdict" and "the harness's limit" is what makes the stop trustworthy instead of accidental.
